Sources, limits and downloads

Elenz historical series are built from Electricity Authority metered generation (where published), dispatch data for recent periods, and satellite-modelled embedded solar. The public Elenz feed is CC BY 4.0 with attribution. Results should be treated as indicative, not direct household-meter measurements.

· Embedded-solar methodology · Detailed past half-hourly generation at Elenz

Important accounting: embedded generation is gross output, not necessarily all electricity directly self-consumed. Grid exports and displacement of grid imports are separate quantities. Comparisons are descriptive, not a claim that every embedded MWh displaced fossil generation. Weekly totals may be partial at the beginning/end of a selected date window. The page shows up to the latest data available from its active source.
